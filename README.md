# hubble-tension-geometry

A geometric alternative to early dark energy models that resolves the Hubble tension using a transient, hyper-stiff confinement-release energy spike derived from higher-dimensional brane rupture dynamics.

## Overview

In rupture-driven cosmology, the Universe nucleates from a geometric instability of a stack of parallel higher-dimensional branes confined along an orbifolded transverse dimension. 

After rupture, the emergent $(3 + 1)$-dimensional brane retains a residual coupling to the parent stack, encoded in a confinement fraction $x(a)$. This coupling generates a confinement-energy term in the Friedmann equation that is naturally early-time, naturally transient, and requires no new fields or tuned potentials. 

Using a physically motivated confinement-energy function $f(x) = x^{\alpha}(1 - x)^{\beta}$, the resulting energy density exhibits a localized, transient hyper-stiff energy spike ($w \gg 1$) prior to recombination. This reduces the sound horizon and raises the CMB-inferred value of $H_0$.
